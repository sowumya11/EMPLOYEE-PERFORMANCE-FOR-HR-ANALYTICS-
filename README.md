Employee Performance for HR Analytics | Power BI Project

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing employee data using Microsoft Power BI to understand workforce distribution, employee performance, KPI achievement, 
training outcomes, and employee experience. 
The analysis helps identify meaningful HR insights and supports better data-driven decision-making.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze employee demographics across departments, regions, gender, age, and education.
- Evaluate employee performance ratings and KPI achievement.
- Understand training participation and training score patterns.
- Examine employee experience based on length of service.
- Analyze awards and employee recognition across departments.
- Generate insights to support effective HR planning and decision-making.

📊 Dashboard Highlights

1. Workforce 360° Dashboard

- Total employee count.
- Employee distribution by department and region.
- Gender, age group, and education analysis.

2. Performance & KPI Insights Dashboard

- Average previous-year performance rating.
- KPI achievement rate.
- Performance rating distribution.
- Department-wise KPI achievement and awards analysis.

3. Training & Development Dashboard

- Average training score.
- Number of trainings attended.
- Training score by department and education.
- Training participation and score analysis.

4. Employee Experience & Achievement Dashboard

- Average length of service.
- Employee distribution by years of service.
- Awards won by department.
- Performance rating by length of service.
- Employee age vs. length of service.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI – Data visualization and dashboard development.
- Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation.
- DAX – Creating calculated measures and KPIs.
- Microsoft Excel / CSV – Dataset handling and preparation.

🔍 Key Insights

- Identified workforce distribution patterns across departments and regions.
- Evaluated employee performance and KPI achievement trends.
- Compared training scores across departments and education levels.

Generated insights to support HR planning and employee development.
